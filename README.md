# dumpster-diving-question-list
A few questions to ask while looking at similar games on Steam etc.





# SEARCH & STEAM PAGE

## What draws me in? Why did I click?

## At what point does the marketing lose me?

## At what point does the marketing draw me in?

## Does anything make me suspicious that this game is waste of time/money?

## What's one thing that this page could do differently?

## Does this game stand out?



# WHILE PLAYING

## What do I feel when this game launches?

## What does the menu make me feel?

## What does the tutorial make me feel?

## At what point are you suspicious this game might be wasting your time or money?

## At what point does the game lose you completely?

## What minimal changes to stand out?

## What minimal changes to fun?

## What could the developer remove?

## Is there something y ou feel like the developer spent a lot of time on that is hard to understand or unwieldy?

## Any game mechanics not working together?



# AFTER PLAYING

## How did this  happen?

## How many people make this game?

## What skills do they have?

## Are they funded?

## What skills do they have that my team lacks?

## What skills do I have they lack?

## Would you play this game?

## Would you play your own game?
